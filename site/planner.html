Standalone: reads no bot state and places no orders. Legs are priced at the mid of the live chain (editable) — Alpaca when your keys are set, otherwise Nasdaq / Yahoo. The mark curve and the Greeks are Black-Scholes at each leg's IV, rate 4 %; the mark carries the options, the held shares (core + adjustment) and the ladder's inventory as rungs fill against it, no ladder round trips. Bold red verticals: where the chosen date's mark turns negative. Bold green: the NET target and where that date's mark reaches it. Verdict: viable = spot inside a profitable zone with ≥ 1 one-day σ (spot × mean IV / √252) of room to the nearest bleed level both ways; tight = under that on a side; not viable = under 0.5 σ, or marked to lose at spot. Max loss (±Nσ, default 2) = the worst of that same mark, net of the spread cost, for any price within ± N one-σ moves of spot over each date's horizon (σ = mean leg IV × √(days/365), lognormal) on every date to expiry; past the band the loss keeps growing at the "per $1" rate shown (no stop-loss by rule). Win rate = probability of a profit at expiry under a lognormal at the legs' mean IV. Recalculate recomputes from the legs, grid and adjustment as they stand; with "recalc as I type" off (the default, remembered in this browser) edits only mark the button pending until you click it. The same figures as JSON: POST /api/payoff.
