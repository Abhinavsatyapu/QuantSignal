## 2. Rules found (and how often the sheets follow them)

| # | Rule | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| R1 | The four legs are **one debit vertical + one credit vertical on opposite sides**: *bullish* options = call **debit** spread + put **credit** spread, or *bearish* options = put **debit** spread + call **credit** spread. | 48 bullish, 29 bearish, 1 inconsistent (page 13, swapped strikes) |
| R2 | The perp **follows the signal** (owner, 26 Oct 2026) and the options hedge the other way: bullish signal → **LONG** perp + bearish options; bearish signal → **SHORT** perp + bullish options. | perp opposite to the options in 77/78 |
| R3 | **Lots: both variants** (owner, 26 Oct 2026): every run computes **1C:2P and 2C:1P** for each perp side and recommends one with transparent scoring (theta, capital, skew, trend, what-if robustness, sheet frequency). Sheets: LONG perp 2C:1P 25/30, 1C:2P 5/30; SHORT perp 1C:2P 45/48, 2C:1P 2/48. | 77/78 use one of the two (other: 5 (5c/6p)) |
| R4 | Debit spread long leg = **first out-of-the-money strike** (median 0.10σ, 1.02% from spot). | median |error| of the rule 0.89% of spot |
| R5 | Debit short strike ≈ **0.90σ** from spot; credit short strike ≈ **0.63σ** on the other side; credit wing ≈ **0.47σ** beyond the credit short. σ = spot × ATM IV × √(days/365), IV solved from the sheet premiums (median ATM IV 76%). | rule within 1% of spot: debit short 28/74, credit short 42/74, wing 28/74 |
| R6 | **Scenario prices are the two short strikes**: UPSIDE DISTANCE = short call − entry, DOWN DISTANCE = entry − short put. | up 69/78, down 73/78 |
| R7 | **Perp quantity equalises the TODAY estimated P&L at the two short strikes**: q = (OptDownToday − OptUpToday) / (up + down distance), then **rounded down to a multiple of 5** (owner, 26 Oct 2026; 72/78 sheet quantities are multiples of 5). This is a chord delta over the ±short-strike band, slightly smaller than the point delta-neutral hedge. | median |error| 5.9% vs. the exact value; 14.1% vs. delta-neutral; 16.7% vs. equalising the *expiry* P&L |
| R8 | Estimated net = option P&L + perp P&L − TOTAL MM LOSS (basket / market-making allowance, $60 median). | 68/78 reproduce to $5 |
| R9 | Capital (owner, 26 Oct 2026): **futures margin = FQ × entry / 2**, 5× leverage; total = futures margin + options capital. Perp notional median $21050; the sheets' INVESTMENT ≈ 0.88 × notional and is **not** reproduced exactly by any tested formula (section 2b); the equalised expiry P&L at the short strikes is the "target" (median $912). | descriptive |
| R10 | Trades were **closed early** (median 2 days, before expiry); booked P&L median $200 + MM P&L median $52; return on investment median 1.37% per trade. All 37 recorded trades are CLOSED with positive booked P&L. | 37 closed trades |

**Engine defaults** (public/strategy-engine.js `REFERENCE_DEFAULTS`): debit long 0.1σ OTM, debit short 0.9σ, credit short 0.63σ, wing 0.47σ, lots by signal (bullish 2 calls : 1 put, bearish 1 call : 2 puts; overridable), perp = equalising quantity rounded down to a multiple of 5, futures margin = notional / 2, leverage 5×, theta flagged when the net is a debit beyond 15% of gross, MM allowance $50. All are configurable in the builder's Advanced panel. Per ticker, the server recalibrates the σ distances from that ticker's sheets when it has ≥3 rows, and records realised outcomes for later calibration.

### Reproduction accuracy (whole dataset)
* Expiry option P&L recomputed from the transcribed strikes/quantities/premiums (pure intrinsic value): **18/78** rows within $25 of the printed values. The rest differ because of blurred premiums or edited variants (flagged per row below) — not because of the formula: the net-P&L formula reproduces 68/78.
* Perp quantity, rule R7 applied to the sheet's own TODAY P&Ls (rounded down to a multiple of 5): median |error| **4.3%**, 49/78 within 5%, 69/78 within 10%.
* Perp quantity, rule R7 with **our** Black-Scholes valuation of an immediate move (IV solved from the premiums; what the live engine does): median |error| **5.9%**, 54/73 within 10%. The gap vs. the line above is the sheet's own "TODAY" model, which we cannot see (likely a broker analyser with different IV per strike / time).
* Strike rule R4/R5 on a standard strike grid: median |error| debit long 0.89%, debit short 1.08%, credit short 0.87%, wing 1.09% of spot (≈ one strike step); all four strikes exact on 1/74, ≥3 exact on 8/74. These σ distances are in-sample medians; the sheets' strike choice is a discretionary band rather than a fixed formula.

## 4. How the engine works now (Strategy Builder · optimizer + what-if)
Inputs are only **ticker, expiry and target** (per lot set × lot sets); everything else is automatic and overridable under *Advanced*.
1. **Data**: the real chain for the expiry (Alpaca when keys are set, otherwise Cboe public delayed quotes, retried 3× with a clear message on failure). If the expiry is not listed, the nearest listed one (≥ 1 day) is used with a warning. Liquidity filter: nonzero bid, not crossed, bid/ask ≤ 25% of mid, open interest ≥ 10; fewer than 3 usable calls or puts → refused as illiquid. Hedge: the Hyperliquid US-stock perp, else **shares** (warned); a perp/stock price gap > 5% is refused.
2. **Direction** from the app's own indicators (15m Hyperliquid candles): a LONG/SHORT signal sets the perp side; on WAIT the lean (EMA trend + MACD momentum + RSI) decides, and with no lean both sides are compared. The reason is shown; Advanced can force Bullish / Bearish / Both.
3. **Search**: debit long −0.15…0.4σ, debit short 0.4…1.5σ, credit short 0.3…1.3σ, wing 0.2…1.0σ beyond, × both lot variants × perp side(s). Fill price = mid ± haircut × half-spread (default 0.5). Lots: fixed at the chosen lot sets (per-lot-set mode) or the smallest size reaching the total (fixed mode). Perp = today's-P&L equalising quantity at the short strikes, **rounded down to a multiple of 5**.
4. **Score** (lower is better; weights stored and versioned): fit to target (residual), capital, risk within ±2σ, theta, liquidity, reference-sheet σ geometry, IV skew, probability of the target zone, then what-if robustness and tail. Every candidate considered is saved with its factors.
5. **What-if before any recommendation** (top 12 + best of each side/variant): price grid −10…+10% plus every strike, both exits and breakevens × today / mid-life / 1 day before / expiry; IV shocks −20…+20% and a −40% crush × price (heatmap); theta and the flat-price path; tail (max loss and where, ±2σ/±3σ), lognormal probabilities (finish/touch each exit, profit, target) and expected value; execution (mid vs haircut vs full spread, fees, margin, current funding to expiry); earnings / ex-dividend dates before expiry (Nasdaq public calendar, best effort).
6. **Gates** (refuse rather than force a pick): P(profit) ≥ 35%, 2σ loss ≤ 4× target, EV ≥ −50% of target, ≥ 25% of pre-expiry what-if cells profitable, and the **account limits** — max loss per trade (worst P&L inside ±2σ, default $2,000 = 2%), capital per trade ≤ 60% of the account, and the remaining weekly loss budget (default $5,000 = 5%, reduced by this week's realised losses). If nothing passes: "No structure meets the target with acceptable risk", with the reasons.
7. **Scaling**: a lot multiplier rescales legs, perp (re-floored to 5), target, capital and the full what-if instantly on the same quotes, and flags risk-cap, capital and open-interest breaches (order > 25% of OI blocks; > 10% of OI, > today's volume or > displayed size warns).
8. **Weekly Plan**: a diversified liquid universe (≤ 2 per sector, editable), one optimizer run per ticker, rescaled to ×1/×2/×3; greedy selection by reward:risk × P(profit) within capital, per-trade and weekly loss caps, sector caps and a pair-correlation cap (Hyperliquid 1h or Nasdaq daily returns; unknown = 0.8 assumed). Combined outcome by a seeded correlated-lognormal Monte Carlo (20,000 paths): P(week ≥ $3k / $4k), P(profit), EV, 5th percentile, P(loss ≥ weekly cap). It reports how many structures fit when 8–10 do not.

### Optimizer short-strike distance band (26 Oct 2026)
The optimizer no longer places the CE / PE shorts close to spot by default. Each short must be at least **max(minσ × 1σ-to-expiry, min× × 14-week Wilder weekly ATR)** and at most **maxσ × σ** from spot (defaults 1.0σ, 1.0 × ATR, 2.5σ; Advanced → *Min short distance (σ)*, *Min short distance (× weekly ATR)*, *Max short distance (σ)*). Up to 7 strikes per side inside the band are searched independently (asymmetric pairs included). The best candidate of every distinct (side, ratio, CE, PE) pair gets the full what-if, and three what-if factors join the score: **exitFit** (shortfall vs target of the P&L if an exit is hit today, weight 2), **ev** (−EV ÷ target, 1.5) and **pop** (1 − P(profit), 1.5). A wider pair wins only if it scores better. The variant cards show each short's distance in $, % and σ, and a table lists the top 5 pairs (exit payoffs at expiry and today, P(profit), EV, 2σ worst, score). If no liquid strike lies in the band, the farthest liquid strike (≥ 0.3σ) is used and a warning says so. If every pair fails the acceptance gates, the result says which band was searched. The band parameters, chosen distances and top pairs are saved with the run (column `distance_band` and the JSON inputs).

## 4a. Method option: Weekly ATR distances
Selectable in the Builder (Advanced → Method → *Weekly ATR distances*). It changes only **where the two short strikes go**; everything else is the optimizer's machinery.
* **Weekly ATR** = Wilder ATR over N completed weekly bars (default N = 14). True range of each weekly bar = max(H − L, |H − previous close|, |L − previous close|); seed = mean of the first N TRs, then ATR = (ATR × (N − 1) + TR) / N.
* **Bars**: the perp's Hyperliquid candles (the source the indicators use) when the ticker has a perp, otherwise the stock's Nasdaq daily OHLC (or choose either source explicitly). Neither source provides weekly bars here, so weekly bars are built from daily OHLC (Monday-start weeks; the perp's weekend candles fall in the same week). The week still forming is excluded. The ATR value, N, source and the weekly bars used are shown with the result.
* **Short strikes**: CE short = nearest listed call strike to spot + upper × ATR; PE short = nearest listed put strike to spot − lower × ATR (defaults 1.0 and 1.0, editable separately; ties go to the strike farther from spot). Distances are shown in $, % and σ from spot. If a snapped strike fails the liquidity filters, the nearest liquid strike on the same side is used and a warning says so.
* **Optional expiry scaling** (off by default): ATR × √(trading days to expiry ÷ 5), where trading days are NY weekday sessions left (exchange holidays are not removed).
* **Unchanged rules**: the short strikes are the exit / profit targets; the perp follows the signal; 1C:2P and 2C:1P are both evaluated and one is recommended; perp qty equalises today's P&L at the two shorts, rounded down to a multiple of 5; futures margin = FQ × entry / 2 at 5× leverage; theta near-neutral or credit is preferred; debit long = first OTM strike (≈ calibrated 0.1σ) and credit wing ≈ calibrated wing σ beyond the credit short (also 0.35 / 0.47 / 0.6σ tried), as in the reference-sheet method; liquidity filters, account risk caps (max $2k loss per trade by default), per-lot-set targets and scaling, and the full what-if (price grid, IV shocks, theta path, ±2σ/±3σ, POP, EV, slippage, fees, events) with the same acceptance gates.
* **Saved runs** record method `weekly_atr` plus the ATR value, N, multipliers, scaling, source and the chosen strikes (column `weekly_atr` and inside the run's JSON inputs, mirrored to Postgres), and feed the learning loop like optimizer runs. History shows the method of every run.

## 4b. Saving everything and learning
* **Every run is saved** (Builder and every ticker of every Weekly Plan): inputs, market snapshot (spot, ATM IV, IV/bid/ask/OI per leg, quote time and source, indicators, funding, events), all candidates with scores, recommended and chosen structure, what-if summary, lot sets and the reason (`strategy_runs`, `strategy_plans`).
* **Lifecycle**: mark Taken / Not taken, enter entry fills, close with exit fills and the realised P&L.
* **Auto outcomes**: after expiry the market path is replayed (Hyperliquid 1h candles, or Nasdaq daily): the first touch of the CE-short / PE-short exit is valued with Black-Scholes at the entry IVs, otherwise the expiry-close P&L. Untaken strategies become (hypothetical) training data.
* **Learning loop** (every 30 min and on every closed trade): weights are re-tuned from outcomes (actual ×1, auto ×0.5; ≥ 8 needed) as weight × clamp(1 + corr(factor, shortfall vs projection), 0.5, 2). A change **appends a new model version** (never overwrites); the UI shows the model version, projection error (mean / MAE), exit hit rates and slippage per spread bucket.
* **Storage**: SQLite working store, mirrored row-by-row to Postgres (Neon) when `DATABASE_URL` is set and restored on boot; JSON/CSV export and JSON import in the UI.
