Advanced overrides
Optimizer · sheet baseline + optimizer search + what-if

Stage A: the sheet baseline is the 4 strikes of the rule learned from the reference sheets (debit long = the first strike beyond spot; debit short, credit short and wing at the sheet distance for this ticker, blended σ / × weekly ATR), and it always gets the full what-if. Stage B: the optimizer searches around it (both ratios, the sides the signal allows) and runs the full what-if on the top candidates (price grid, IV shocks, theta path, ±2σ / ±3σ, P(profit), EV, slippage, fees, events). The score weighs target fit, EV, P(profit), 2σ worst, theta, capital and a moderate sheet-similarity prior. The optimizer pick is recommended only when it clearly beats the baseline.

Weekly ATR distances

The CE and PE short strikes are the exit / profit targets. Everything else (perp side from the signal, both lot ratios, perp qty floored to 5, margin, theta, long legs as in the reference method, risk caps and the what-if) runs as for the optimizer.
