Signal rules and exit management

Trend: close above/below SMA200 and SMA50, with EMA20 above/below EMA50. Momentum: MACD(12,26,9) histogram agrees with direction. Require at least 6 of 8 checks: trend, MACD, RSI14 (long 45–70 / short 30–55), volume, EMA20 slope, ADX/DMI, rolling VWAP20 and OBV trend. Trend, MACD and ADX ≥ 20 with directional agreement are mandatory. The completed candle must agree with direction, its body must occupy at least 20% of its range, and its close must be in the upper 45% for LONG or lower 45% for SHORT. Otherwise WAIT. Bollinger Bands(20,2) provide context.

Reference entry is the latest completed candle close; entry tolerance is the wider of 0.5 ATR14 and 25% of the planned stop distance. Stop is the wider of 3 ATR, a confirmed swing with 0.5 ATR buffer, and a configurable 1.5% default price floor. TP1 = 2R; TP2 = 3R; TP3 = 4R. Reject setups without 2R room before a confirmed opposing swing. Exit at SL or target, or reassess after a completed candle reverses trend and MACD. If current price moves beyond that entry tolerance, wait for a retest. Fees, slippage, and funding are excluded. Rule agreement is not a win probability; this strategy has not been backtested.

HYPERLIQUID · VIRTUAL EXECUTION

$100,000 Paper Trading

Automatically tests US stock perp signals with 5× leverage. Fresh qualified signals enter at the observed live mid plus modeled slippage; stop loss, take profit and opposite signals close positions. No exchange orders or account keys.

Execution controls

READY

Your position limit and stop inputs save automatically when changed. Targets and stops are planned levels; paper entries fill at the observed live price plus modeled slippage.

Leverage is fixed at 5×. New entries require at least 2R net of modeled fees/slippage; choose 2R, 3R or 4R exits. Stops use volatility and confirmed swing structure with a configurable price-distance floor. A confirmed opposing swing before the selected target blocks entry. Position limits support 1–50 stocks, with quantity reduced to fit remaining margin. Existing positions retain their original levels. Live venue quantity increments are respected. Pausing stops new entries; SL/TP monitoring depends on the selected execution engine remaining active. The watchlist rotates continuously with paced candle requests; fresh signals are rechecked for entries on each 30-second price refresh. Unused margin remains available when no setup qualifies. Prices are polled every 30 seconds, so gap and intrapoll fills can differ from real execution. Partial limits use current visible book depth; full SL/TP exits use observed mids with modeled slippage. Funding, liquidation, queue priority and intrapoll price paths are not modeled. Browser execution is best effort in hidden tabs and stops if the app suspends or closes. Server mode requires a separately hosted runner and continues independently of the browser.

70% limit-exit rule · one time per position

When absolute gross unrealized P&L reaches 10% of initial entry margin, place a virtual reduce-only GTC limit to close 70% of initial quantity, rounded down to the venue increment. A $5,000 initial margin triggers at +$500 or −$500. With an unchanged fill price, 70% realizes approximately +$350 or −$350 gross before costs; the other 30% remains open. Pending orders require fresh matching bid/ask depth; simply touching the threshold does not guarantee a fill.

Filled quantity releases its margin and allocates entry fees proportionally; exit fees are charged once. Original SL/TP safety exits take priority. After the partial limit completes, the remaining position waits for SL, TP or a manual exit; opposite-signal exits are disabled for that runner. Turning the rule off cancels pending partial limits. Tiny positions that cannot retain a valid runner are visibly skipped. Existing positions migrate without resetting their ledger.

Released margin can fund new qualified signals. Each runner still occupies one position slot: increase Maximum positions if you want additional tickers. This rule realizes losses as well as profits and does not guarantee a better return. This app sends no exchange orders.
